The Hook

A sophisticated digital home for a wellness brand specializing in the Renata França method, emphasizing luxury and serenity.

Role & Timeline

Role: Lead Developer
Timeframe: 2 Weeks

The Stack

WordPress Elementor PHP Responsive Design

The Business Challenge

The client needed to translate their high-end physical services into a digital environment. The goal was to build trust and authority while making service discovery and appointment booking as seamless as possible for a luxury clientele.

The Strategic Solution

I utilized WordPress and Elementor to create a highly visual, fluid experience. I implemented custom CSS to achieve the specific soft-green brand aesthetic and optimized the mobile experience to ensure 100% functionality for users on the go.

Measured Results

The site established a professional online presence that perfectly aligns with the brand's premium identity, resulting in a streamlined inquiry process and increased brand visibility in the French wellness market.
