Lyon MMA Center

Mixed Martial Arts training center landing page built with WordPress.

← Back to Portfolio

The Hook

A bold and energetic digital platform for a premier combat sports academy in Lyon, France.

Role & Timeline

Role: Lead Developer
Timeframe: 10 Days

The Stack

WordPress Elementor Branding

The Business Challenge

The academy needed a site that conveyed authority, discipline, and energy while making it extremely easy for potential students to find class schedules and sign up for trials.

The Strategic Solution

I opted for a dark theme with deep burgundy accents to evoke a sense of power and focus. The layout was optimized for speed, ensuring that class schedules and contact forms loaded instantly.

Measured Results

Direct trial sign-ups increased significantly within the first month. The site's SEO ranking for local martial arts keywords in Lyon improved, driving organic traffic to the center.
